    Glossary — modes, agencies, terms
    • Microlet — 14-seat licensed minivan; principal urban mode in Dili (906 registered, 2023).
    • Angguna — medium truck with bench seating; informal first/last-mile carrier (97 registered).
    • Keliling — on-street cruising for passengers instead of using terminals.
    • MOTC Ministry of Transport and Communications · DNTT National Directorate of Land Transport · LTA proposed Land Transport Authority (legislation pending) · MPW Ministry of Public Works · ADB Asian Development Bank · MOP Ministry of Planning and Territory — ADN (National Development Agency) and MPS (Major Projects Secretariat) sit under it · MOI Ministry of Interior · NPC National Procurement Commission.
    • DED — detailed engineering design (US$1.5M budget line for selected terminal designs).
    • Municipal Public Transport Hub — highest-order facility combining regional bus and microlet interchange (Becora, Airport Transit Hub, Manleuana Market, Baucau).
    • Tier 1 / Tier 2 assessment — the plan's two-stage site screening to a composite score out of 5.

    The master plan does not just build infrastructure — it assigns every function to a counterpart. Under the plan's hybrid operating model (§8.3), a strengthened public regulator plans, licenses and monitors while professionalised private operators run the services, and a third band of responsibilities is shared or negotiable case-by-case. This view maps who does what: the institutions and their current mandates (§3.5.2, Table 3.26), the public–private split of functions (§8.3.2), and the reform moves that reassign capacity over time (Tables 8.4–8.5).

    The public sector sets policy, regulates and contracts; the private sector operates services under licence, permit or contract. What is left — vehicles, depots, facilities, information, ITS, revenue — is deliberately shared or negotiable, settled case-by-case as capacity grows. Delivery hinges on two strengthened counterparts: the new department inside the Land Transport Authority, and organised operator associations replacing today's individual owner-operators.

    MOTCMinistry of Transport and Communicationslead ministry

    TodayAccountable across the plan's RACI for vision, route planning, licensing, fares, monitoring and facilities; hosts DNTT and oversees the port, airport and communications authorities.

    ReformPolicy, regulation and contracting move into the LTA's Public Transport Department; MOTC remains the sector's sponsor in Government.

    DNTTNational Directorate of Land Transportunder MOTC

    TodayThe working regulator: routes approved case-by-case, operators licensed, vehicles inspected; keeps the vehicle-registration database — the sector's only digital system.

    ReformStaff and functions seed the new Department; timetable and scheduling capacity is the first build (Moves 02, 10).

    LTALand Transport Authoritynew · legislation pending

    TodayOutlined in earlier reform work, but the enabling laws have not been submitted or passed; establishment has not progressed.

    ReformHosts the Public Transport Planning & Regulation Department; regional offices in municipal centres as functions devolve (Move 03).

    MPWMinistry of Public Worksinfrastructure

    TodayAccountable for highway planning and delivery, and for bus-stop provision that is not delivered; roads lose quality quickly after construction.

    ReformDNTT's counterpart on road improvement for inter-city reliability (Move 06); delivers modern infrastructure with maintenance programmes (Move 14).

    MOPMinistry of Planning and Territoryplanning

    TodayCentral planning body; responsible for highway planning alongside MPW; consulted on facility planning and design.

    ReformNone assigned — highway planning continues with MPW; ADN and MPS sit under MOP.

    ADNNational Development Agency (Agência de Desenvolvimento Nacional)under MOP

    TodayConsulted on facility planning, design and construction in the RACI.

    ReformNone assigned — consulted as the facility programme moves from planning to delivery.

    MPSMajor Projects Secretariatunder MOP

    TodayAppraises major projects for the Infrastructure Fund on technical and financial grounds (Decree Law 8/2011); consulted on facility design and construction.

    ReformNone assigned — facility investments keep the existing appraisal route.

    MOIMinistry of Interiorenforcement

    TodayAccountable for traffic management and enforcement; the road-safety directorate (DNSR) sits under it; traffic police (PNTL) do the on-street work.

    ReformLaw-enforcement training and inter-agency collaboration (Move 01); keliling and over-capacity operation are the named targets.

    NPCNational Procurement Commissionprocurement

    TodayAccountable for supplier procurement (Decree Law 14/2011); no coordinated procurement for public transport assets has happened.

    ReformCounterpart for infrastructure procurement and contract management (Move 14); vehicle-procurement capacity builds alongside (Move 13).

    OPSPrivate operators — microlet + regional bus owner-operatorsprivate sector

    TodayOwn, procure and operate the vehicles, collect the fares, hold maintenance responsibility without enforcement — as individuals, not companies.

    ReformProfessionalise into associations, absorb timetabled operations and service-quality licence conditions, then compete for contracts (Moves 05, 07–09, 12).

    ASSOCOperator association(s)to be formed

    TodayDo not exist; flagged in earlier LTA establishment work but never progressed.

    ReformThe Government's single counterpart: forums first (Move 05), then collaborative governance and collective bargaining (Moves 07, 09).
