    Build rule

    • Every value extracted from the attached plan and recomputed in code; route sums reconciled against the plan's own totals (regional trips 123/168/202/203; investment 17.16/14.53/3.45M; grand total 35.14M) before display.
    • Derived figures, clearly labelled: microlet + airport express boarding sums; 90.2% and 48.8% phase shares; +31.6% ridership growth; 1,494 km network (207.2 + 90.4 + 1,196.6); 74-site count (24 facilities + 50 stops).
    • Scenario semantics: Existing = survey baseline · Immediate = 2025 refinements · Upgrade = 2035 full recommendations · Visionary = 2035 + conventional bus conversion.

    Data lineage

    • Population — 2022 Census (ES.1) · Traffic/mode share — Arup screenline surveys, Apr–May 2023, weekday PM peak (T3.4–3.5).
    • User perception — ≈1,500 residents surveyed, Dili and Baucau (§3.4.5).
    • Fleet — DNTT: 906 microlets (2023), 290 regional buses (2021), 97 angguna (2021).
    • Routes — route-by-route surveys and modelling, §6.4–6.5 · Costs — Arup order-of-magnitude estimates (T9.1–9.3) · Sites — T7.31, 7.37.
    • Freshness: static 2024 plan snapshot; no implementation actuals in source — status flags are calendar-derived and provisional.

    Limitations & confidence

    • Costs are order-of-magnitude (plan's own caveat); ridership is modelled, not measured.
    • Known source gaps: no implementation actuals, no fare revenue data, Tasitolu interchange has no cost line, terminal sites may change per MOTC decision (note to ES.7/ES.8).

    Glossary — modes, agencies, terms
    • Microlet — 14-seat licensed minivan; principal urban mode in Dili (906 registered, 2023).
    • Angguna — medium truck with bench seating; informal first/last-mile carrier (97 registered).
    • Keliling — on-street cruising for passengers instead of using terminals.
    • MOTC Ministry of Transport and Communications · DNTT National Directorate of Land Transport · LTA proposed Land Transport Authority (legislation pending) · MPW Ministry of Public Works · ADB Asian Development Bank.
    • DED — detailed engineering design (US$1.5M budget line for selected terminal designs).
    • Municipal Public Transport Hub — highest-order facility combining regional bus and microlet interchange (Becora, Airport Transit Hub, Manleuana Market, Baucau).
    • Tier 1 / Tier 2 assessment — the plan's two-stage site screening to a composite score out of 5.
